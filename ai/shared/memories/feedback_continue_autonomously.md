Continue autonomously through design, planning, implementation, and verification. Stop only for destructive actions, missing authority, or materially ambiguous choices.

**Why:** The user prefers Claude-like automatic execution and does not want routine workflow approval gates.

**How to apply:** Treat ordinary design and plan decisions as part of execution. Report progress without asking for confirmation unless one of the explicit stopping conditions applies.
